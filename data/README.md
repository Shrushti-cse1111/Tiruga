# Tiruga Travel Catalog & Kannada Companion Data

This directory contains verified reference data for Tiruga (ತಿರು ಗ) - AI Karnataka Travel Companion:

- `destinations.json`: 15 curated Karnataka travel destinations with bilingual names, districts, travel tags, recommended days, and food highlights.
- `attractions.json`: 68 points of interest, temples, waterfalls, and heritage spots with coordinate grids (`mapX`/`mapY`), estimated costs, and indoor shelter flags.
- `phrases.json`: 56 travel phrases in 7 categories (Basic conversation, Restaurant, Transport, Directions, Hotel, Shopping, Emergency).
  **Note on Kannada script & phrasing:** The phrases are written in standard formal and colloquial Kannada script (Noto Sans Kannada). A native Kannada speaker must review them before enterprise production deployment to ensure local conversational nuances match specific regional dialects (e.g. Uttara Kannada vs Mysuru Kannada vs Hyderabad-Karnataka).
- `transport.json`: 18 demo transport routes across major Karnataka travel corridors with realistic duration, stops, and price estimates.
